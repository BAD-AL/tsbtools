QUESTION
  Does GENESIS_FIX_SIM_BUGS (defense roll fix) make team defense SimData count like it does on NES?

WHAT IS DIFFERENT IN THIS ROM
  Baseline (every team, unless changed below): QB [rush 4, pass 8, pocket 3],
  RB/WR/TE [rush 8, catch 8, yards/catch 8, targets 8], defenders [pass rush 30, coverage 30],
  K/P 8, team SimData 0x88, run/pass preference 0. Names and in-game ratings are stock.
  Change: Same as GEN_05b (Team SimData A = 0x00, B = 0xF0, C = 0x0F, D = 0xFF, preference 0), plus the GENESIS_FIX_SIM_BUGS game fix.

GROUPS
  A  (SimData 0x00): bills, jets, steelers, chargers, eagles, lions, falcons
  B  (SimData 0xF0): colts, bengals, broncos, seahawks, cardinals, packers, rams
  C  (SimData 0x0F): dolphins, browns, chiefs, cowboys, redskins, vikings, saints
  D  (SimData 0xFF): patriots, oilers, raiders, giants, bears, buccaneers, 49ers

HOW TO RUN
  1. Load the ROM in the emulator with fresh save data (delete any old .sav / battery file first).
  2. Start a season and set all 28 teams to be simulated (SKP / computer, as you normally sim a season).
  3. Simulate the full regular season (fast-forward is fine).
  4. Record the season stats below from the team / player stats screens.
     Short on time? Record at least 2 teams per group (8 teams) -- that is usually enough to see the pattern.

WHAT TO EXPECT
  - Bigger group differences than GEN_05b, closer to NES_05: high digit cuts rush yds allowed (NES A-D 2353/1431/2417/1440),
  - low digit cuts pass yds allowed and adds sacks/INTs; pts allowed NES 486/418/424/324 (GEN_05b 397/358/362/346).
  - Also compare league rush yards with GEN_05b: Genesis run-play tiers are stingier than NES, so runs may come out short.
  - Save an ares state (.bs1) after the season: yards and points allowed can be checked from it.
