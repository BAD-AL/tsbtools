QUESTION
  How much does the QB's Sim pass change completions, yards, sacks and interceptions? (Open questions 1 and 4)

WHAT IS DIFFERENT IN THIS ROM
  Baseline (every team, unless changed below): QB [rush 4, pass 8, pocket 3],
  RB/WR/TE [rush 8, catch 8, yards/catch 8, targets 8], defenders [pass rush 30, coverage 30],
  K/P 8, team SimData 0x88, run/pass preference 0. Names and in-game ratings are stock.
  Change: QB1/QB2 Sim pass = 0 / 5 / 10 / 15 by group. All teams preference 3 (70% pass).

GROUPS
  A  (QB Sim pass 0): bills, jets, steelers, chargers, eagles, lions, 49ers
  B  (QB Sim pass 5): colts, bengals, broncos, seahawks, cardinals, packers, rams
  C  (QB Sim pass 10): dolphins, browns, chiefs, redskins, cowboys, vikings, saints
  D  (QB Sim pass 15): patriots, oilers, raiders, giants, bears, buccaneers, falcons

HOW TO RUN
  1. Load the ROM in the emulator with fresh save data (delete any old .sav / battery file first).
  2. Start a season and set all 28 teams to be simulated (SKP / computer, as you normally sim a season).
  3. Simulate the full regular season (fast-forward is fine).
  4. Record the season stats below from the team / player stats screens.
     Short on time? Record at least 2 teams per group (8 teams) -- that is usually enough to see the pattern.

WHAT TO EXPECT
  - Completion % should rise from group A to D.
  - If INTs thrown or times sacked also change with Sim pass, the QB value feeds into whether those happen.
  - If sacks/INTs are flat across groups, they are decided by something else (see NES_05 / NES_07).
