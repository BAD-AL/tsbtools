QUESTION
  Do the team SimData digits control rush defense (high) and pass defense (low)? Do they affect sacks and interceptions? (Open question 4)

WHAT IS DIFFERENT IN THIS ROM
  Baseline (every team, unless changed below): QB [rush 4, pass 8, pocket 3],
  RB/WR/TE [rush 8, catch 8, yards/catch 8, targets 8], defenders [pass rush 30, coverage 30],
  K/P 8, team SimData 0x88, run/pass preference 0. Names and in-game ratings are stock.
  Change: Team SimData: A = 0x00 (rush 0, pass 0), B = 0xF0 (rush 15, pass 0), C = 0x0F (rush 0, pass 15), D = 0xFF. Preference 0 for all.

GROUPS
  A  (SimData 0x00): bills, jets, steelers, chargers, eagles, lions, 49ers
  B  (SimData 0xF0): colts, bengals, broncos, seahawks, cardinals, packers, rams
  C  (SimData 0x0F): dolphins, browns, chiefs, redskins, cowboys, vikings, saints
  D  (SimData 0xFF): patriots, oilers, raiders, giants, bears, buccaneers, falcons

HOW TO RUN
  1. Load the ROM in the emulator with fresh save data (delete any old .sav / battery file first).
  2. Start a season and set all 28 teams to be simulated (SKP / computer, as you normally sim a season).
  3. Simulate the full regular season (fast-forward is fine).
  4. Record the season stats below from the team / player stats screens.
     Short on time? Record at least 2 teams per group (8 teams) -- that is usually enough to see the pattern.

WHAT TO EXPECT
  - Rush yds allowed: low for B and D, high for A and C.
  - Pass yds allowed: low for C and D, high for A and B.
  - If sacks/INTs made follow the low digit (C, D high), pass defense decides whether they happen.
  - KR yds allowed low for B and D would confirm the high digit also acts as kick coverage.
